= Rooms and floors

Group devices so the dashboard mirrors your home. A device belongs to at most one room, and a room
to at most one floor. Grouping is only an organizing layer; a device works the same whether or not
it is assigned.

.The *Rooms* page: devices placed on a floor plan.
image::rooms.png[Devices grouped by room on a floor plan]

On the *Rooms* page:

. *Add floor* for each floor of the home, then *Add room* to place rooms on it.
. Drag a device from *Unassigned* onto a room to assign it, or between rooms to move it.
. *Rename* or *Delete* a room or floor from its controls.
